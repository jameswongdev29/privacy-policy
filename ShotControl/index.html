Privacy Policy

Effective Date: May 22, 2026

ShotControl ("the App") is an iOS app that provides professional manual camera controls — ISO, shutter speed, focus, white balance — along with monitoring tools such as histogram, focus peaking, and zebra stripes. We respect your privacy. This policy explains what data the App does and does not handle.

Data We Collect

None. The App does not collect, transmit, store on our servers, or share any personal information or usage data. The App does not require an account, does not connect to any analytics service, and does not include any third-party SDKs.

Network Connections

ShotControl makes no outbound network connections. All image processing — including histogram computation, focus peaking, and zebra stripe rendering — runs entirely on your device using Metal shaders. No telemetry, no auto-update checks, no background sync, no analytics, no remote config.

Camera and Microphone

The App accesses your device camera and microphone solely to provide its core photography and video recording features. Camera frames are processed on-device for live preview and real-time monitoring overlays. No images, video, or audio are transmitted off your device by the App.

Photo Library

When you capture a photo or video, the App saves it to your device's Photo Library using Apple's Photos framework. The App does not read, upload, or modify existing photos in your library.

Location

If you grant location permission, the App may embed GPS coordinates in photo EXIF metadata — a standard practice in camera apps. Location data is stored only in the photo file on your device. You can disable this at any time in iOS Settings. The App does not transmit location data to any server.

In-App Purchases

ShotControl offers a one-time Lifetime Unlock (non-consumable in-app purchase) after a free trial period. All purchases are processed by Apple's StoreKit. We do not receive, store, or transmit your payment method or any personally identifying information. From StoreKit, the App reads only the cryptographically signed transaction receipt that Apple issues (product identifier, purchase date, original transaction identifier) so it can unlock paid features locally and support the "Restore Purchases" button. Billing and refund handling are governed by Apple's Standard EULA and your App Store account settings.

On-Device Storage

The App stores the following on your device only:

  • Your preferences (exposure mode, grid type, monitoring toggles, video settings).
  • Trial start date, used to determine free trial eligibility.
  • Purchase status, verified locally via StoreKit.

This data lives on your device. It is removed when you delete the App. We have no access to it.

Widget Extension

The App includes a Golden Hour widget that calculates sunrise and sunset times using your device's location and astronomical algorithms. The calculation runs entirely on-device. The widget does not perform any network requests and does not transmit anything off the device.

Children

The App does not knowingly collect any information from children or any other users.

Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
