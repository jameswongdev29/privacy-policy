SleepWave Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-09

SleepWave is developed by James Huang Studio, an independent developer. This policy explains the information handled by the app, including optional syncing through your own Apple iCloud account.

Developer data collection

We do not operate a developer backend for SleepWave. The app contains no advertising, tracking, or third-party analytics SDKs. We do not collect or sell your sound mixes, imported recordings, playback history, or other personal information on developer-operated servers. SleepWave does not require a separate developer account or login.

Apple provides App Store purchases and optional iCloud services. Those services involve connections to Apple and are subject to Apple's privacy policies. Local playback does not require an internet connection once the selected audio is available on the device.

Information stored by the app

InformationStorage and purpose
Saved sound mixes, volume levels and sound variantsApp Group UserDefaults allows the app and its Home Screen widget to share favorites. When iCloud is available, favorites also sync through your own iCloud key-value store.
Imported custom audio and its metadataStored in your own iCloud Drive container, iCloud.com.jameshuangstudio.sleepwave, when available, with a local Documents/CustomSounds fallback. iCloud syncing can transfer these files and their names between your devices.
Playback-session summariesStored in app-local UserDefaults for Sleep Insights. These summarize audio playback, not measured sleep stages or medical data.
Last playback selectionStored in app-local UserDefaults, including sound choices, volume levels and applicable timer configuration, to let you choose to continue a previous mix. The app does not automatically start that mix on cold launch.
Timer, wake-up, language, appearance and sound preferencesStored locally to remember your settings.
Three-day trial startStored in this device's Keychain to determine trial availability. The trial record may remain after reinstalling the app.
Purchase entitlementThe app asks Apple's StoreKit to verify lifetime access and to purchase or restore that access. Apple manages the signed transaction records and their local cache.

Imported audio remains under your Apple account's storage and sharing settings when iCloud is used. We do not receive the contents of your iCloud files. Availability and syncing depend on your device, Apple account, iCloud settings and network connection.

Purchases and restoration

The app offers a three-day full-feature trial and an optional one-time lifetime unlock. StoreKit displays the price for your storefront. Apple processes the purchase, payment and restore requests; the app does not receive your payment-card details. Restoring purchases requires communication with Apple and may require Apple Account authentication.

Audio, notifications and permissions

SleepWave uses background audio so selected sounds can continue when the screen is locked. If you enable Smart Wake, the app can request notification permission and schedule local wake-up notifications. It does not register for remote push notifications.

The app does not access HealthKit, record your microphone, or request camera or location access. Sleep Insights is a playback log and does not diagnose, monitor or treat a medical condition.

Managing your information

You can remove favorites and custom sounds in the app. Removing custom files or changing iCloud settings can affect their availability on your other devices. You can manage iCloud storage and notification permissions in the device's Settings. Apple Account purchases and Apple-held data are managed through Apple.

Children

SleepWave has no advertising or developer-operated personal-data collection and is not specifically directed at children under 13.
